Introduction

Welcome to Jamia Ilmiya Imam Hassan Askari. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use this website.

We respect your privacy and aim to provide a safe, transparent and useful Islamic educational experience.

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the website, certain information may be provided voluntarily or collected automatically by website technologies.

Information You Provide

If you contact us through a contact form or email, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address and message.

Automatically Collected Information

Basic technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location and pages visited may potentially be collected by hosting, analytics or advertising services.

How Information May Be Used

Information may be used for legitimate purposes such as:

  • Responding to questions and messages.
  • Improving website content and functionality.
  • Understanding general website usage.
  • Maintaining website security.
  • Detecting and preventing misuse.
  • Providing relevant website features.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website or third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies.

Cookies can help websites remember preferences, understand website traffic and provide certain features.

Your browser may allow you to control or disable cookies through its settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website features.

We may use third-party services for purposes such as website hosting, analytics, security or advertising.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Users should review the privacy policies of third-party services when appropriate.

Data Retention

Information submitted through the website may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, subject to applicable requirements.

If you believe that a child has submitted personal information through the website, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information, including rights to access, correction, deletion or restriction of certain processing.
