Islamic Prayer Times

Daily Salah timings and useful information about the five daily prayers.

Daily Salah

Salah is one of the fundamental acts of worship in Islam. Muslims perform five obligatory prayers throughout the day and night.

Prayer times are determined according to the position of the sun and can vary by location, date and calculation method.

The Five Daily Prayers

1. Fajr — الفجر

The prayer performed during the dawn period before sunrise.

2. Dhuhr — الظهر

The prayer performed after the sun passes its midday point.

3. Asr — العصر

The afternoon prayer.

4. Maghrib — المغرب

The prayer performed shortly after sunset.

5. Isha — العشاء

The night prayer performed after twilight.

Friday — Jumu'ah Prayer

Jumu'ah

Jumu'ah is the congregational Friday prayer performed in place of the regular Dhuhr prayer for those upon whom it is obligatory.

The exact Jumu'ah time should be confirmed with your local mosque or Islamic authority.

Prayer & Salah Guidance

  1. Make sincere intention for the prayer.
  2. Perform Wudu when required.
  3. Face the Qiblah.
  4. Perform the prayer according to the established Islamic method.
  5. Pray within the appropriate prayer time.
  6. Maintain concentration, humility and sincerity during Salah.

About Prayer Time Calculations

Prayer times are calculated using astronomical information such as the position of the sun. Different calculation methods may produce slightly different times.

Local mosques and Islamic authorities may also follow specific timetables or adjustments.
