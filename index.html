About Jamia

Preserving the legacy of Imam Hassan Askari (AS)

Our Mission

Jamia Illmia Imam Hassan Askari is a centre of Islamic learning, dedicated to nurturing scholars who embody the teachings of Ahlul Bayt. We combine traditional Islamic sciences with modern education to prepare students for leadership and service.

"Knowledge is the heritage of the prophets." — Imam Hassan Askari (AS)

350+ Alumni
28 Faculty
12 Countries

Our Programs

Islamic sciences · Arabic · Fiqh · Philosophy · Ethics

Qur'an & Tafsir

Deep study of revelation, exegesis, and memorization with traditional methods.

Fiqh & Usul

Jurisprudence and principles of Islamic law, with comparative analysis.

Philosophy & Logic

Islamic philosophy, logic, and intellectual discourse from early to modern.

Seerah & History

Life of the Prophet (PBUH) and the Ahlul Bayt, Islamic civilization.
