About Hadith

Hadith refers to reports describing the sayings, actions, approvals and characteristics of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

The study of Hadith is an important part of understanding Islamic teachings and the practical example of the Prophet ﷺ.

Featured Hadith

إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ

"Actions are judged by intentions."

Source: Sahih al-Bukhari; Sahih Muslim

Major Hadith Collections

  • Sahih al-Bukhari — A major collection of Hadith.
  • Sahih Muslim — A renowned collection of authentic Hadith.
  • Sunan Abu Dawud — A well-known Hadith collection.
  • Jami` at-Tirmidhi — A recognized collection of Hadith.
  • Sunan an-Nasa'i — One of the major Hadith collections.
  • Sunan Ibn Majah — A widely referenced Hadith collection.

Learning from Hadith

  1. Read Hadith carefully and consider its context.
  2. Check the source and authenticity of a narration.
  3. Learn from trusted scholars and established Islamic references.
  4. Apply beneficial teachings with wisdom, sincerity and good character.

Important Note

Hadith authentication and interpretation require careful scholarly study. Before sharing or acting upon a narration, verify its wording, source, authenticity and context through reliable Islamic references or qualified scholars.
