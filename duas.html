Islamic Duas & Supplications

Discover beneficial Duas from the Quran and authentic Islamic tradition for everyday life.

About Islamic Duas

Dua is a form of supplication through which a believer asks Allah for guidance, mercy, forgiveness, protection and blessings.

This section provides a collection of commonly recited Duas and Quranic supplications. Visitors should verify detailed references through reliable Islamic sources.

Featured Dua

Dua for Good in This World and the Hereafter

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

"Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire."

Reference: Quran 2:201

Duas for Forgiveness

Dua for Forgiveness

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَارْحَمْنِي

"My Lord, forgive me and have mercy upon me."

This short supplication expresses a believer's request for Allah's forgiveness and mercy.

Quranic Supplication

رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَا أَنْفُسَنَا وَإِنْ لَمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ

"Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers."

Reference: Quran 7:23

Reference: Quran 1:6

Daily Duas

Dua Before Eating

Remember Allah and begin your meal with gratitude and the name of Allah.

Dua After Eating

Thank Allah for the food and blessings provided to you.

Dua Before Sleeping

End the day with remembrance of Allah, gratitude and sincere supplication.

Dua When Leaving Home

Ask Allah for guidance, protection and blessings when beginning your journey.

Etiquette of Making Dua

  1. Begin by praising and remembering Allah.
  2. Send blessings upon the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
  3. Ask Allah sincerely and with humility.
  4. Have hope in Allah's mercy and wisdom.
  5. Avoid asking for something harmful or sinful.
  6. Continue making Dua with patience and sincerity.
